Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Clip Farm is an internal tool operated by the owner of acoethan.com. It creates short video highlights from public Twitch livestreams and publishes them to YouTube channels owned by the operator. This policy explains what data the tool accesses and how it is used.

1. Google / YouTube data

Clip Farm uses the YouTube Data API with the youtube.upload and youtube.readonly scopes only: to upload videos to YouTube channels owned by the operator, and to read view, like and comment counts for those videos. Clip Farm does not read, collect, store or share any other Google user data, and does not access viewers' accounts.

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the operator's own computer and are never transmitted to any third party. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Clip Farm's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Clip Farm uses YouTube API Services; by using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Twitch data

Clip Farm reads public Twitch chat messages in real time to measure audience engagement (message rate and common phrases). Chat messages are processed in memory and are not stored, except for a short, anonymous summary phrase saved with each clip. Clips are created through the official Twitch API and are credited to and linked back to the original streamer.

3. Data retention

Rendered video files are deleted from the operator's computer within a few days of publication. A local log records each clip's ID, title, engagement score and YouTube video ID.

4. Sharing

No personal data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is only sent to Google (YouTube) and Twitch as needed to provide the service.

5. Content removal

Streamers or rights holders who would like a clip removed can contact us and it will be taken down promptly.
