Clip Farm

Clip Farm is our internal tool. It finds the biggest moments on Twitch streams (with the streamers' permission through their clipping programs), adds captions, and publishes them as YouTube Shorts on channels we own. Every Short credits and links back to the original streamer.

Clip Farm uses YouTube API Services only to upload videos to, and read view statistics for, our own channels. It doesn't collect data from viewers.

Streamers or rights holders who want a clip removed can email ryan.leong888@gmail.com, and we'll take it down promptly.
